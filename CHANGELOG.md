# Changelog

Versions follow the [Google Fonts versioning scheme](https://googlefonts.github.io/gf-guide/requirements.html).
The full per-version notes for 1.x–2.5 are in
[documentation/design/RELEASE-NOTES-1.x-2.5.txt](documentation/design/RELEASE-NOTES-1.x-2.5.txt).

## Unreleased
- FontBakery (googlefonts profile): 21 FAIL to 0 FAIL, 51 WARN to 39. Scripts in `scripts/history/v2.502/`.
- Build: overlaps removed with skia-pathops (booleanOperations cannot handle the quadratic
  sources); the small-caps family build is off; `scripts/hint.py` adds a STAT table and
  rewrites the webfonts from the hinted TTFs, so the `.woff2` files now carry hinting and STAT.
- Metadata: WinDescent 389 so no glyph is clipped, vendor ID `ARBR`.
- Glyphs: added U+2212 MINUS SIGN, U+2183 ROMAN NUMERAL REVERSED ONE HUNDRED and U+A7CB LATIN
  CAPITAL LETTER RAMS HORN, and U+24B6 Ⓐ and U+24D0 ⓐ (circled A and a, which
  Koalib needs; built on the copyright sign's oval); removed U+00AD SOFT HYPHEN.
- Mark positioning: top and bottom anchors on about 200 letters per style that had none
  (Latin Extended-D and -E, Cyrillic Extended-B, Greek and phonetic additions), so combining
  accents attach to them; `center` anchor on U+25CC DOTTED CIRCLE; six combining marks now
  classed as marks.
- Small caps: `smcp` and `c2sc` now cover 165 more letters (Latin Extended, IPA, Vietnamese
  and other accented letters), so small-cap text no longer drops back to lowercase.
  Turkish, Azerbaijani and Crimean Tatar get a dotted small-cap i.
- Soft-dotted letters (i, j, і, ј and the rest) lose their dot under every above-mark in the
  font, including when a below-mark comes between them.
- Accent placement: a letter typed with a separate combining accent now matches the
  precomposed letter. Anchors were refitted from an ink comparison of every precomposed
  letter (the grave, double grave and double acute had sat 30–50 units off centre); the
  ogonek has its own anchor so it stays at the letter's right foot.
- Small caps: the small-cap combining accents were full-width spacing glyphs, so accents
  typed separately in small-cap text landed a whole cell to the right; they are now
  zero-width marks. Off-centre accents on nine small-cap letters (ǩ ȟ ḣ ṳ ṵ ụ ạ ậ ặ) are
  centred, and the dot of small-cap ḋ no longer hides inside the D.
- Spacing: letters re-centred in their cells by optical side space (mean white space across
  the full height, measured against o or O; half the offset corrected). j moves 13–25 units
  right (its hook made it look pushed left, as in `jumps`), J 6–7 right; in the uprights also
  d C G +5/+6, B D −5 (Regular) and k +5 (Bold); in the italics t +23, k +15/+16, I +9/+13 and
  y −22/−23. Accented forms and identical Cyrillic and Greek letters move with their base,
  anchors included. The italic capitals' larger measured offsets (V, T, Y, U, L) were left
  alone: the slant biases the measurement (the symmetric H reads −9).
  ȷ ĵ ǰ ј ϳ ɉ share j's stem; ĵ and ǰ in the uprights had sat about 40 units left of j, and
  their accents now match a typed j + combining accent.
- Hinting: ttfautohint now reads per-style control files in `sources/hinting/`, generated by
  `scripts/make_hint_controls.py`. U+210A ℊ was lifted 0.37 em at every size and U+25CC ◌
  moved 2 px at 10 px; both are now scaled without distortion. Accents that hinting pushed
  onto their letter (mostly Cyrillic and Greek dots and diaeresis, and accented letters at
  11 px) are kept 1 px clear at the sizes where that happened, 71–106 letters per style,
  in FreeType's v35 and v40 interpreters. Ẫ Ễ Ỗ in Regular and Bold still touch at 16–21 px,
  because lifting them would clip under Windows GDI. FreeType's pedantic mode reports no
  bytecode errors at 7–72 px.
- `meta` table (design and supported scripts: Latin, Greek, Cyrillic), added by `scripts/hint.py`.

## 2.501 — 2026-11-15
- First public repository. Added UFO sources (generated from the 2.501 binaries and checked
  against them), a gftools builder config, CI and documentation.
- Metadata: USE_TYPO_METRICS set (line spacing unchanged), Mac name records removed, license
  URL, copyright and designer fields updated, empty `liga` and `kern` features removed.

## 2.500
- Hinting: Bold now matches the other styles' x-height, cap height and ascender at every size
  from 8 to 48 px. The integer-ppem flag is set. Two glyph programs that failed in FreeType's
  pedantic mode (U+1ABF, U+1AC0) were removed.

## 2.400
- Upright H: straightened the pinched stems in Regular and Bold.
- About 300 accented, Greek and Cyrillic glyphs per style were re-synced to their base letters.
- Italic and Bold Italic: stroke weight raised to match the uprights (H, N, M, W, m, w, D, K,
  U, Cyrillic П И Ш …).
- "Bold Italic" style name corrected; all four styles carry the same version.

## 1.2 – 2.3
- Monospaced adaptation of Noto Serif: stroke-aware condensation, capital spacing, italic
  capital and figure revisions, I/J and diagonal-letter refinements. See the release notes
  linked above.
