NOTO SERIF MONO LINING — 2.3

A compact monospaced, optically balanced derivative of Noto Serif.
The four styles are Regular, Bold, Italic and Bold Italic.
All printable ASCII glyphs use 563/1000-em advance width.
Latin, Greek and Cyrillic letters use stroke-aware condensation where needed.
The narrow t, r, i and l extend their serifs, not vertical stems.
Zero is diagonally slashed; O is not. Lining uses Noto's zero.slash.
Numbers are tabular and cap-height lining.
Embedded TrueType auto-hint programs cover 7–72 ppem.
No pair kerning changes character advances or column alignment.

Install all four TTFs by selecting them in Windows and choosing Install.
The distinct Noto Serif Mono and Noto Serif Mono Oldstyle families can coexist.
Source: Google Noto Serif 2.004, installed OFL-licensed release.
Noto Serif copyright 2015 Google LLC; see OFL.txt and SOURCE_COPYRIGHT.txt.

Version 2.1 is a three-pass spacing review for both slanted faces.
Each pass rerendered 24 sample words and strings at 16, 21 and 30 px/em.
Pass 1 refines cap terminals on I, J, L and F. Pass 2 addresses i,
r and t in mixed-case samples and refines I's left serif. Pass 3
refines W and A across WA, E in VE and OE, and J's left serif.
The archive includes the two intermediate comparisons, the final
comparison, and per-pair median horizontal clearances for each pass.

The cap-line IJ gap narrowed 32 units in both faces (y=685), and the
median WA gap narrowed 10 units in Italic and 12 in Bold Italic.
Some sampled gaps grow when the same glyph participates in another
pair, including eJ at mid-body. All advances were preserved. The
final scan found no new or worsened sampled intersections among
8,836 non-space printable ASCII pairs per face. FreeType figure
bitmaps matched at all six tested sizes. To reproduce, run
SOURCE-v2.1/pass_spacing.py with the source directory, an existing
output directory and pass number, feeding each output into the next.
Start from a directory containing only the two v2.0 slanted TTFs.

Version 2.3 audits stroke weight across 2,840 mapped characters in
each of the four faces. Weight-inventory.csv reports raster ink
coverage and density before and after at 96 px/em, with matching
Noto Serif coverage as a reference. Ink coverage is a screening
measure and depends on the shape and the amount of compression.

Stroke checks and proofs identified light outer stems in M, related
loss in N and W, and narrow strokes in lowercase m. These strokes
were strengthened into existing counters in both slanted faces,
including contour-matched accented and cross-script forms. Italic U
also received a small correction. A Bold Italic U trial was removed
after worsening clearance with 7. See NotoSerifMono-Stroke-Weight.png.

All advances and mappings are preserved. The final scan found no
new or worsened sampled intersections across 8,836 printable ASCII
pairs per slanted face. Figures rendered identically at six tested
FreeType sizes. Windows DirectWrite was untested.

Version 2.4 is a consistency and stroke-weight release for all four
faces. Advances, cmaps, glyph order, point counts and hint programs are
unchanged; only point positions moved.

1. Upright H. The inner edges of both H stems in Regular and Bold were
   pinched toward the crossbar (Regular 94/83/77/92 units at
   y=150/300/420/560; Bold 130/123/115/133). They are now vertical:
   96 and 135 units at every height. The fix carries to Η and Н.
2. Accented forms re-synced. In 1.3 and earlier, many precomposed
   letters had been condensed separately from their base letter and had
   drifted by up to 30 units (Regular Ù/Ú 26, Ñ 23, Ď 28, Ģ 30). The
   base-letter contour inside 290–380 glyphs per face is now an exact
   copy of the plain letter, and marks move with its centre. Greek and
   Cyrillic look-alikes (А В Е Н К М Т О Р С Х, Α Β Ε Ζ Η Ι Κ Μ Ν Ο Ρ
   Τ Υ Χ and lowercase а е о р с х у і ј ѕ) follow the Latin letter.
   Apostrophe-caron forms (ď ľ ť Ľ), ŀ/Ŀ, tonos capitals and polytonic
   Greek were left alone because their bases are intentionally offset.
3. Italic and Bold Italic weight. The slanted faces had been condensed
   much harder than the uprights while keeping larger counters:
   Italic H stems were 66 units against Italic I at 87 and n at 92.
   Stems are now thickened horizontally with the bounding box kept
   fixed. N, M, W and w thicken only their backslash (thick) strokes,
   keeping the thin strokes thin. Every change is propagated to
   contour-matched accented, Greek and Cyrillic forms.
     Italic       H 66→83  N 47→79  M 37→63  W 58→72  D 75→83  K 67→80
                  m 74→85  w 67→77  П 65→89  И 62→78 (+ Д Ж Л Ц Ш Щ Ы Ю Я Ф К Γ …)
     Bold Italic  H 102→119  N 63→94  M 48→63  W 80→102  D 107→119
                  m 104→116  w 98→106  П 91→112 (+ Γ Б Д Ж Л Ц Ш Щ …)
     Bold         D 127→139
   Counters were not allowed to shrink below 72% of their previous
   width or 22 units, whichever is larger.
4. Naming. Style name "BoldItalic" is now "Bold Italic" (IDs 2 and 4).
   Redundant IDs 16/17 removed. All four faces are version 2.400
   (Regular and Bold previously reported 1.300). OS/2 vendor ID changed
   from GOOG to NONE, since this is not a Google release.

Verification (Version-2.4-proof/Verification.txt): no new or worsened
overlaps across 8,836 printable ASCII pairs per face, using 76 sampled
rows from y=-240 to 885. No contour changed direction. FreeType hinted
renders at 12, 16 and 20 px were inspected. DirectWrite was not tested.
The hint programs were not regenerated because ttfautohint was not
available. They still address the same points, but a rehint is
recommended.

To reproduce, run SOURCE-v2.4/build24.py OUTPUT and then finalize.py
OUTPUT. build24.py reads the v2.3 TTFs from the path set in its source.

Version 2.5 is a hinting audit and repair release. Outlines, advances
and metrics are identical to 2.4. The ttfautohint programs (from the
original builder) were examined with a FreeType 2.14 harness that
renders every glyph unhinted, hinted v35, hinted v40 and monochrome,
including FreeType's pedantic bytecode mode (SOURCE-v2.5/ftr.c).

Findings that were fixed:
1. Bytecode errors. The glyph programs for U+1ABF and U+1AC0 raised
   Invalid_Reference (0x86) at 7–32 ppem in all four faces (2–3 glyphs,
   680 failures in total). Their instructions are removed; they now
   render from the plain outline. The pedantic scan is now clean for
   all glyphs at 7–72 ppem in v35, v40 and mono.
2. Bold did not line up with the other faces. ttfautohint measured
   Bold's x-height overshoot as 549 units (548 in a second entry)
   against 546 in Regular, Italic and Bold Italic. Because the hints
   scale each glyph to fit the rounded x-height, Bold was scaled
   differently at many sizes. Bold keywords in Regular code were taller
   or shorter by a pixel at 8, 16, 17, 25, 26, 28, 32, 33 and 38–48 px.
   Bold's five x-height alignment CVT entries (47, 67, 141, 161, 181)
   now use the Regular values. All four faces render the same
   x-height, cap height, ascender and figure height at every size from
   8 to 48 px. See Version-2.5-proof/NotoSerifMono-v2.5-MixedStyleAlignment.png.
3. head.flags bit 3 ("force integer ppem") was clear. The OpenType
   spec strongly recommends it for hinted fonts and it is now set.

Checked and left unchanged:
- Blue zones: baseline, x-height, cap height, ascender and figure
  heights align within each face at every size from 9 to 24 px in all
  renderers. Hinted vertical extents match the outlines. Neither the
  later outline edits nor the 2.4 changes left stale hints.
- X-height rounding: ttfautohint rounds the x-height up from .375 px.
  This scales glyphs up by as much as 6% at some sizes and makes heights
  step in pairs (16/17, 21/22 px). Round-to-nearest only shifted where
  the steps fall (patch_round.py), so the default was kept.
- Stem width mode stays "natural". The "strong" mode, which can be
  enabled with patch_mode.py, makes horizontal strokes crisp, full-black
  single pixels at 9–14 px. It also adds 10% ink to Regular and Italic
  and closes more counters in the slanted faces. See
  Optional-StemModes-natural-quantized-strong.png.
- Windows GDI clipping: a few stacked-accent capitals (Ǜ Ṍ Ḗ Ẳ…) lose
  one pixel at the top at 10, 12 and 14 px. Some symbols also extend
  past the descent line (ℊ U+0489 U+A670–A672). Raising usWinAscent
  and usWinDescent would increase GDI line spacing, so the metrics
  were kept.
- A full rehint with current ttfautohint is still worthwhile if it
  becomes available. Run ttfautohint first, then apply hint25.py, so
  the CVT harmonisation and fixes 1 and 3 are preserved.

Build: python3 SOURCE-v2.5/hint25.py <2.4 dir> <out dir>

Version 2.501 contains metadata cleanups found by a FontBakery-style audit.
FontBakery itself could not be installed in the build environment.
SOURCE-v2.5.1/minibakery.py reimplements about 45 key checks from its
OpenType, Universal and Google Fonts profiles using fontTools. It is
not a substitute for a real FontBakery run.
Outlines, advances, hint programs and CVT are byte-identical to 2.500.

Fixed: USE_TYPO_METRICS (fsSelection bit 7) is set. Typo, hhea and win
values are already equal, so line spacing does not change. Mac
(platform 1) name records were removed. The license URL is now
https://openfontlicense.org. The empty GSUB 'liga' and GPOS 'kern'
features were removed.

Still reported: usWinDescent 293 is less than the lowest glyph,
ℊ U+210A at -389. Six more glyphs also extend below -293:
U+A670–A672, U+0489, U+2E10 and U+2E11. Windows GDI clips these.
Raising usWinDescent to 389 would make GDI line spacing taller and was
not done. The Google Fonts copyright-string format warning applies only
to Google Fonts submissions.
