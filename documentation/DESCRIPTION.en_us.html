Noto Serif Mono is a monospaced serif for code, terminals and technical writing. It is an adaptation of Noto Serif in which every glyph shares a 0.563 em advance. Wide letters are condensed with stroke-aware scaling, narrow letters are re-spaced to fill the cell, and the family's stroke weight is balanced so that Bold keywords sit naturally in Regular text.

The family has four styles and covers Latin, Greek and Cyrillic. It includes small caps, old-style and lining figures, fractions and localized forms, and it is hinted for clear rendering at small sizes on screen.

To contribute, see github.com/wmarklloyd/noto-serif-mono.
